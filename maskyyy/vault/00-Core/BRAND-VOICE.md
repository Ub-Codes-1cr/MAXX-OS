# MAXX OS - Core Brand Voice Directive

## Tone & Style
- Direct, concise, technical, zero fluff.
- High signal-to-noise ratio.
- Authoritative yet accessible.
- No corporate jargon or buzzwords.

## Anti-Words (Banned Globally)
- game-changer
- revolutionary
- synergy
- unleash
- paradigm shift
- disruptive
- secret sauce
- magic

## Content Principles
1. **Local-First:** Prioritize creator sovereignty, privacy, and self-hosted tools.
2. **Value First:** Provide actionable insights, code blocks, architecture diagrams, or concrete metrics upfront.
3. **Platform Masking:** Native tone for each platform. Never copy-paste raw text across channels without adaptation.
